## The problem

I was writing about a blog post with a companion presentation about keyboard shortcuts and the benefits of knowing your environment/tools. This idea sort of developed over time, just in thinking through how to make learning keyboard shortcuts and other helpful commands/macros/automations, more easily accessible and integrated, without constant context-switching or distraction.

## The first attempt

The first version was an IDE plugin designed as a sort of structured 'workshop' or tutorial that I was going to use as my presentation. It was meant to be repeated by a user as many times as they needed to repeat until the commands stick. It covered a lot of ground in different areas (IDE, standard document editing, web browser, git...), but was still limited to those domains and the most commonly used shortcuts within them. I ended up doing a slide presentation instead and didn't develop this idea any further until I started thinking about it again recently. The idea this time has expanded and I'll be rebuilding completely from scratch.
